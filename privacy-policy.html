Privacy Policy

Daily Affirmations · Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Affirmations mobile application ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. We built the app to be privacy-friendly: it has no user accounts, and the affirmations you read and save stay on your device. Please read this policy alongside Google's Privacy Policy, which covers the advertising described below.

No accounts, no sign-in

The app does not require you to create an account or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number. We do not ask you to log in.

Data stored on your device

Your app content — including favorited affirmations, your daily reminder time, which categories you have unlocked, and your last-viewed position — is stored locally on your device only. We do not have servers that receive, store, or process this information, and it is never transmitted to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is permanently removed.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information from your device, including:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as your Advertising ID (a resettable identifier), and device/network information (for example device type, operating system, and general, coarse location inferred from your IP address).
  • Ad interaction data used to deliver, cap, and measure advertisements and to limit repetitive ads.

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's policies. You can learn about and control ad personalization, and reset or delete your Advertising ID, in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (Android). Resetting or opting out of a personalized Advertising ID does not remove ads, but limits ad personalization.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This is handled entirely on-device; no notification data is sent to us or to any server.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is provided by Google in accordance with its policies.

Data security

Because your app content stays on your device, its security is tied to your device's own protections. Any data transmitted for advertising is sent over encrypted connections by Google.

Your choices

  • Reset or opt out of your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Turn off the daily reminder any time in the app's Settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
